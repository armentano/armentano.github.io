Abstract

The main issue faced in this proyect was the lack of control and traceability over the recycling processes in a battery factory, which were managed through printed spreadsheets and manual records. This methodology not only hindered the tracking of material batches and their traceability throughout the production process, but also limited the company’s ability to analyze deviations in production costs, identify failures by series, and accurately evaluate the efficiency of the industrial process.

The development of the system aimed to digitalize each stage of the process, improve the quality of data recording, and enable the generation of strategic reports to support decision-making. Likewise, special emphasis was placed on achieving an intuitive interface for the plant operator, taking into account the specific usage conditions of the industrial environment.
