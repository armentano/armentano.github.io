Abstract

The main objective of this final project was to design and develop a software system capable of predicting the time it would take a person to travel from one specific location to another using the public bus transportation system in the city of Tandil. Additionally, a mechanism was developed to identify the most recommended possible routes between a pair of origin and destination coordinates, based on certain input parameters, aiming to produce reliable results regardless of the many factors that affect vehicle movement.

The proposed system therefore considered not only aspects related to public transportation—such as bus line and route—but also external factors such as time of day, city center traffic, day of the week, and other conditions.

Given the high daily demand for public buses by citizens, the system sought to use the most recent and accurate information available to provide a service that users could trust. With the collected data and the use of various graphs and diagrams, it was possible to gain a clearer and more precise understanding of the performance of the transportation system.

Additionally, the implementation of a series of metrics allowed for a mathematical analysis of the performance of the proposed models.

During development, special attention was given to ensuring that the data used by both the model and the application did not compromise the privacy of public transport employees, passengers, or any other individuals. Likewise, no information regarding bus infrastructure was stored or disclosed, thus guaranteeing a fully secure and trustworthy system.

At the system architecture level, the goal was to create a structure that was compatible with the widest possible range of mobile devices, regardless of their technological specifications. Furthermore, a simple and user-friendly interface was designed to ensure that the user experience was intuitive, appealing, and pleasant, while also encouraging new users to adopt the application.
