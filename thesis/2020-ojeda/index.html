Abstract

The general objective of this research is to examine whether the exchange rate of Bitcoin reflects people’s opinions about the digital currency. To explore this, it is important to establish whether previous research has shown that investors are influenced by sentiment regarding other traditional investments, such as stocks. It is also relevant to examine the characteristics of investments that are more susceptible to public commentary, and whether those same characteristics apply to Bitcoin. Furthermore, since this paper uses publicly available data from the internet, it is important to assess the reliability of that source and where it has been previously used, particularly in relation to financial markets.

The proposal of this work consists in the development of approaches for predicting currency trends, for which Machine Learning techniques will be applied, as they offer the ability to learn from data and allow the software to evolve automatically.

This proposal is based on the general objective of the project, which is to develop a tool that provides the necessary support to make informed speculations about monetary trends, using Bitcoin as a case study.

The tool to be developed will use trust metrics from the analyzed posts, taking into account user characteristics (such as whether the account is verified, number of followers, geographic location, etc.) and characteristics of the post itself (keywords, sentiment analysis, whether the comment is positive or negative, etc.).

After the analysis is performed, each post (a tweet) will be assigned a score to support the speculation of the trend.
