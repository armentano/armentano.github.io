Online Harm-Aware Recommendation Systems in Social Networks

Dec 1, 2022
Recommender Systems Online Social Networks

The spread of disinformation and hate speech on social media via the Internet has become a critical global issue. In recent years, there has been a notable increase in both the dissemination of fake news, rumors, deception, and other forms of disinformation, as well as the use of abusive language, incitement to violence, harassment, and other types of hate speech. Both aspects contribute to what is known as “online harm,” which can have potentially severe consequences for individuals and society, ranging from public health issues to the disruption of democratic systems.

This project focuses on defining new algorithms and recommendation systems aimed at mitigating the negative effects of harmful online content. The main objectives of this project are:

Defining new methods for learning user profiles that model their preferences and behavior regarding malicious content by analyzing heterogeneous information from social media platforms. Defining and categorizing users based on their role in the spread of online harm. Designing novel harm-aware recommendation system approaches that incorporate mechanisms to counteract the effects of harmful content in social networks.
