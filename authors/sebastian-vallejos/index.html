He have experience using Natural Language Processing techniques and Machine Learning algorithms. In the past, he have applied these techniques to short and informal texts extracted from social media to classify and extract useful information on specific topics—particularly, extracting information related to traffic incidents.

Along similar lines, he have also used these techniques for the automatic classification of user-generated content on e-commerce platforms, aiming to predict the category of published products based on their descriptions.

His most recent and most interesting work focuses on the use of mobile devices for learning about urban mobility in medium-sized cities. Specifically, techniques were applied to regulate the collection of sensor data from mobile devices without negatively impacting battery life.
